About PKCERT

Pakistan's National Cyber
Defense Frontline

PKCERT operates under the National Telecommunication Corporation (NTC) as the national Computer Emergency Response Team. Established to protect Pakistan's critical information infrastructure, PKCERT coordinates cyber security incident response and provides technical assistance to government organizations, critical infrastructure operators, and the broader cybersecurity community.

As a member of the global CERT community, PKCERT collaborates with international partners to share threat intelligence and coordinate cross-border incident response activities.
